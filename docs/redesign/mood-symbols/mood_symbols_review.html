Mood symbols, refined set

Five original Living Landscapes illustrations for the check-in mood scale. Values, order and labels are exactly those in check_in_screen.dart: 1 Very Low, 2 Low, 3 Neutral, 4 Good, 5 Great. The progression is carried by rain quantity (9 → 3 → 0), cloud size and weight, and sun presence (none → partial → full), so it does not depend on colour.

1 · Very Low

Large dense dark cloud, 9 heavy rain strokes, dark wash

2 · Low

Smaller lighter cloud, 3 sparse drizzle marks

3 · Neutral

Low cloud bank, 3 mist lines, no rain, no sun

4 · Good

Dominant sun (7 rays) emerging behind a small cloud, no rain

5 · Great

Large open sun, 12 full rays, gentle hill, widest glow

Actual mobile size

360 px phone width, 20 px gutters, 40 px symbols in five equal tiles (about 59 × 96 px each). Viewed at 1× this is true size.

Selected and unselected states

Selection is shown by four cues, none of them colour alone: border weight (1.5 → 2.5 px), a check badge, a paper fill with an offset print shadow, and label weight (500 → 600). Screen readers announce each tile as a radio, for example “Low, 2 of 5, selected”.

Measured distinctness (grayscale render)

Each symbol rendered by Chrome at 1×, converted to grayscale, measured per pixel. “Marked” is any pixel noticeably different from parchment; “dark ink” is luminance under 110. Pairwise difference is the mean absolute luminance difference (0 to 255). This is a coarse, shape-blind measure. Low vs Good is still the lowest-scoring pair, because both are light symbols of similar overall weight. Their difference is in kind (drizzle marks vs a ringed sun with rays), which this number does not capture, so judge that pair by eye in the grayscale rows above.

Accessibility checklist

  • Written labels kept under every symbol; symbols are decorative to screen readers (empty alt), the tile carries the name.
  • Accessible names: “Very Low, 1 of 5”, “Low, 2 of 5”, “Neutral, 3 of 5”, “Good, 4 of 5”, “Great, 5 of 5”, plus “selected”.
  • Touch targets about 59 × 96 px at 360 px width and about 51 × 96 px at 320 px (20 px gutters, 6 px gaps; minimum 48 × 48).
  • Selection is never colour-only: border weight, check badge, fill and label weight.
  • Symbols use only paths, clip paths and opacity, so flutter_svg can render them, with no filters, patterns or fonts.
  • Not yet done: integration, Flutter widget tests, real VoiceOver and TalkBack passes (outstanding project-wide).
